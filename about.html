About Us

Integrated Fruits & Vegetables Processing Solutions

Who We Are

Raja Food & Agro Innovations is a professionally managed integrated fruits and vegetables processing unit, established to deliver high-quality, hygienically processed food ingredients and products for domestic and international markets.

Our facility integrates multiple advanced processing technologies under one roof, enabling complete control from raw material handling to finished product dispatch. This integrated approach ensures superior quality consistency, traceability, operational efficiency, and food safety compliance.

We operate with a strong focus on B2B partnerships, OEM manufacturing, private labeling, and job work processing, supporting food manufacturers, FMCG brands, nutraceutical companies, exporters, and HoReCa clients.

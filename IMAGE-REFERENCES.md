## 🎨 Image Guidelines

### Quality Standards
- High resolution (minimum 1920px width for heroes)
- Professional photography preferred
- Good lighting and clear focus
- Consistent color palette (greens preferred)

### Content Guidelines
- Show modern, clean facilities
- Focus on food safety and hygiene
- Include safety equipment and clean uniforms
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds
- Showcase technology and equipment

### Optimization
- Compress images to reduce file size
- Use JPG for photos, PNG for logos
- Target < 500KB per image
- Maintain aspect ratios

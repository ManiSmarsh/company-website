Quality Control Process

1
Raw Material Inspection

Thorough inspection and testing of all incoming raw materials for quality parameters, contaminants, and compliance

2
In-Process Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of critical process parameters during production to ensure consistency

3
Laboratory Testing

Comprehensive testing in our in-house laboratory for physical, chemical, and microbiological parameters

4
Finished Product Testing

Final product evaluation against specifications before release for packaging

5
Packaging Inspection

Verification of packaging integrity, labeling accuracy, and storage conditions

6
Documentation & Traceability

Complete record-keeping and batch traceability from raw material to finished product
